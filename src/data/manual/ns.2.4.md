## What it is

Multiplying rational numbers is the integer rule from 2.1 with fractions and decimals in the mix. The sign works exactly the same way; the only new part is getting the numbers into a form you can multiply.

**Key idea: decide the sign first. Convert mixed numbers to improper fractions. Simplify, then multiply straight across.**

## How to solve it

1. Decide the sign first, before any arithmetic. Count the negative factors: an even count gives a positive answer, an odd count gives a negative one.
2. Turn every mixed number and decimal into an improper fraction, so everything is one shape.
3. Simplify across the multiplication before you multiply. Cancel a factor on any top with a factor on any bottom, so the numbers stay small.
4. Multiply straight across, top times top and bottom times bottom, and write the answer with the sign from step 1.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** multiplying the whole parts and the fraction parts separately, so 2 1/2 × 3 1/2 comes out as 6 1/4. **Fix:** convert to improper fractions first. 5/2 × 7/2 = 35/4 = 8 3/4.
- **Mistake:** getting the arithmetic right and losing the sign. **Fix:** decide the sign in step 1 and write it down before you multiply, so it cannot go missing at the end.
- **Mistake:** cancelling a top and a bottom inside the same fraction. **Fix:** cancelling means dividing a numerator and a denominator by the same factor across the multiplication sign. Inside one fraction that is just simplifying, and it does not help here.

## Where it shows up

- **Refuel and escort** — a fraction of a tank burned on each of several legs.
- **Intercept sorties** — thrust as a fraction of maximum, held for part of a minute.
- **Systems:** Engine, Fuel, Weapons.
- **Real aircraft:** the T-38 holds two crew. Loading 3/4 of a full fuel state for each of 2 sorties is 3/4 × 2 = 3/2, a tank and a half in total.
