## What it is

Pour a pyramid into the box with the same base and height and it fills exactly a third of it. That one fact is the whole skill.

**Key idea: V = (1/3) × B × h. A pyramid or cone holds one THIRD of the prism or cylinder with the same base and height.**

## How to solve it

1. Find the base area, the same way as for a prism.
2. Multiply by the HEIGHT — how tall the pyramid stands, straight up. Not the slant, which runs up the sloping face.
3. Now take a THIRD of it. That is the whole difference between a pyramid and the box around it.
4. Going backwards, multiply the volume by 3 and then divide by the base area.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** leaving out the one third, so a pyramid comes out as big as its box. **Fix:** three of these pyramids fit inside that box. Divide by 3.
- **Mistake:** using the slant height in the volume. **Fix:** volume uses the VERTICAL height. The slant belongs to surface area only.

## Where it shows up

- **Hangar** — the volume of a nose cone.
- **Briefing** — a pyramidal ordnance crate.
- **Systems:** Weapons, Fuel.
- **Real aircraft:** a nose cone's volume is worked out as a cone, which is exactly a third of the cylinder behind it.
