## What it is

A graph with no numbers on it still tells a story. Rising, flat and falling are the whole vocabulary, and steeper means faster.

**Key idea: Read a graph as a story: rising means increasing, flat means constant, steeper means faster. Sketch a story the same way.**

## How to solve it

1. Read the graph left to right as a story in time. Going up means increasing, going down means decreasing.
2. FLAT does not mean stopped. On an altitude graph a flat stretch is level flight, still moving forwards.
3. Steeper means faster, not higher. Two rising stretches at different angles are two different rates of climb.
4. The graph is not a picture of the flight path. A rising line on a speed graph is an aircraft speeding up, not climbing.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** reading the graph as a picture of the route. **Fix:** check the axis labels. Altitude against TIME is not a drawing of where the aircraft went.
- **Mistake:** reading a flat stretch as "stopped". **Fix:** flat means that quantity is not changing. At a constant altitude the aircraft is still flying.

## Where it shows up

- **Debrief** — altitude against time for a whole sortie.
- **Briefing** — the shape of a refuel or a holding pattern.
- **Systems:** Avionics, Fuel.
- **Real aircraft:** a flight profile is read exactly this way: climb, cruise, descent, all in one shape.
