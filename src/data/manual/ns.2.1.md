## What it is

Multiplying integers is repeated addition with a sign attached. Three legs of the same descent is the descent rate times three, and if the rate is negative the answer is negative too.

**Key idea: multiply the sizes. Same signs give positive, different signs give negative. An even count of negatives is positive.**

## How to solve it

1. Multiply the sizes, ignoring the signs for now. This is the multiplication you already know.
2. Count how many of the factors are negative.
3. An even count of negatives gives a positive answer; an odd count gives a negative one. Two negatives cancel each other, the way two minus signs do.
4. Write the answer with that sign, and put the units back on.

Watch the brackets on a square. **(−5)² means (−5) × (−5) = 25**, because the minus is inside. **−5² means −(5 × 5) = −25**, because only the 5 is squared.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** using the addition sign rule, so (−4) × (−6) comes out as −24. **Fix:** addition and multiplication have different rules. In multiplication two negatives make a positive: (−4) × (−6) = 24.
- **Mistake:** reading (−5)² and −5² as the same thing. **Fix:** look at what the exponent is attached to. Brackets mean the minus gets squared too; no brackets means it does not.
- **Mistake:** losing count of the negatives in a product of three or more. **Fix:** count them on your fingers before you multiply. Odd means negative, even means positive, every time.

## Where it shows up

- **Intercept sorties** — descent rate times minutes gives total altitude lost.
- **Refuel and escort** — burn rate times leg length gives fuel used.
- **Systems:** Engine, Fuel, Avionics (altitude).
- **Real aircraft:** the T-38 climbs at roughly 33,600 ft per minute at sea level. Four minutes of descent at −4,000 ft per minute is (−4,000) × 4 = −16,000 ft.
