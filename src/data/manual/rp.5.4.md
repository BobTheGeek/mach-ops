## What it is

A proportion is two equal ratios. Writing one down correctly is most of the work: matching units have to sit in matching positions, or the equation says something you did not mean.

**Key idea: match units to matching positions (top with top). Solve with y = kx, a scale factor, or cross products.**

## How to solve it

1. Write the proportion so the same quantity is on top of both fractions, and the same quantity on the bottom of both.
2. Look for a scale factor between the two values you know of the same quantity. If it is clean, multiply the other pair by it.
3. If there is no clean factor, use cross products: multiply diagonally, and set the two products equal.
4. Solve for the unknown, then check that the size and the units of your answer make sense.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** putting the units in different positions, so litres sits over miles on one side and miles over litres on the other. **Fix:** label both fractions before you solve. Top must match top.
- **Mistake:** adding the difference instead of scaling, so 3 : 5 becomes 8 : 10. **Fix:** ratios scale by multiplying. 3 : 5 doubled is 6 : 10.

## Where it shows up

- **Briefing** — fuel for a longer leg, worked out from a known rate.
- **Hangar** — missiles needed for a given number of bogeys.
- **Systems:** Fuel, Weapons.
- **Real aircraft:** the T-38 is 14.0 m long and 7.6 m across. A model 19.4 cm long has its span set by 14.0 / 7.6 = 19.4 / x.
