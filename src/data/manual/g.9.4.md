## What it is

Three lengths do not always make a triangle. Whether they close depends on one simple check, and knowing what FIXES a triangle tells you when a description has one answer and when it has many.

**Key idea: Three sides make a triangle only if the two shorter ones add to MORE than the longest. Three sides fix ONE triangle; three angles adding to 180 fix infinitely many.**

## How to solve it

1. Put the three lengths in order, smallest to largest.
2. Add the two SHORTER ones. They must come to MORE than the longest — not the same as it.
3. Equal means the three lie flat on top of each other. 2, 3 and 5 make a straight line, not a triangle.
4. Three SIDES fix exactly one triangle. Three ANGLES adding to 180 fix the shape but not the size, so there are infinitely many. Angles that do not add to 180 fix nothing at all.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** accepting 2, 3 and 5 because 2 + 3 = 5. **Fix:** it has to be MORE, not equal. At exactly equal the triangle has flattened to a line.
- **Mistake:** thinking three angles pin down one triangle. **Fix:** they fix the shape only. A 30-60-90 triangle can be the size of your hand or the size of a runway.

## Where it shows up

- **Briefing** — whether three patrol legs can close into a circuit.
- **Sortie** — intercept geometry between you, the target and the base.
- **Systems:** Avionics.
- **Real aircraft:** a navigation triangle only closes if the three legs satisfy this rule, which is why a flight plan that does not close has a mistake in it.
