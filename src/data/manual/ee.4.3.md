## What it is

Two things have been done to x, so you undo them one at a time. Reverse the order they were done in: the last thing done is the first thing you take off.

**Key idea: undo addition or subtraction first, then multiplication or division. For p(x + q) = r, divide both sides by p first.**

## How to solve it

1. Read the equation and notice both things being done to x. Undo them in the opposite order to how they were done.
2. Undo the adding or subtracting first, on both sides.
3. Now undo the multiplying, by dividing both sides by the coefficient. If the whole thing sits inside brackets, divide by the outside number first instead.
4. Check by putting the answer back into the original equation.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** dividing before subtracting, so 4x + 12 = 60 gives x = 15 − 12. **Fix:** take the 12 off first: 4x = 48, so x = 12.
- **Mistake:** dividing only the x term, leaving the constant untouched. **Fix:** whatever you divide by divides everything on that side.
- **Mistake:** reading p(x + q) as px + q. **Fix:** the brackets mean p multiplies the whole thing. Either divide by p first, or distribute properly.
- **Mistake:** a sign slip on the constant. **Fix:** write the subtraction under both sides before you work it out.

## Where it shows up

- **Briefing** — time to a waypoint: 4t + 12 = 60.
- **Hangar** — the bill for n missiles plus a fitting fee.
- **Systems:** Avionics, Fuel, Weapons.
- **Real aircraft:** three T-38 tanks plus a 400 lb reserve totalling 3,100 lb gives 3t + 400 = 3,100, so each tank holds 900 lb.
