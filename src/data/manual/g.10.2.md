## What it is

A cylinder is the easiest solid to unfold: two circles and one rectangle. The only thing to get right is how wide that rectangle is.

**Key idea: Unroll it: two circles plus a rectangle whose width is the CIRCUMFERENCE. S = 2πr² + 2πrh.**

## How to solve it

1. Unroll it in your head. A cylinder flattens into TWO circles and one rectangle.
2. The rectangle's width is the circumference of the circle, 2πr — not the diameter, and not the radius.
3. So the curved side is 2πrh, and the two ends come to 2πr² between them.
4. Add them for the total, or stop at the curved part if that is all that was asked for.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** using the diameter as the rectangle's width. **Fix:** the rectangle wraps all the way round, so its width is the distance round — the circumference, which is π times bigger than the diameter.
- **Mistake:** counting only one circular end. **Fix:** a closed cylinder has a top AND a bottom. A label that covers neither uses only the rectangle.

## Where it shows up

- **Hangar** — the label that wraps round a fuel drum.
- **Briefing** — the skin of an external fuel tank.
- **Systems:** Fuel, Engines.
- **Real aircraft:** an engine nacelle is close enough to a cylinder that its surface area is worked out exactly this way.
