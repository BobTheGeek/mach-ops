## What it is

When x has been multiplied by something, you undo it by dividing. When it has been divided, you multiply. A fraction in front of x is undone by multiplying by that fraction upside down.

**Key idea: undo multiplication by dividing both sides by the coefficient; undo division by multiplying. A fraction coefficient: multiply by its reciprocal.**

## How to solve it

1. Find what is happening to x. A number written next to x means it is being multiplied.
2. Do the opposite to both sides. Dividing undoes multiplying; multiplying undoes dividing.
3. If the coefficient is a fraction, multiply both sides by the **reciprocal** — the fraction turned upside down. That is the same as dividing by it.
4. Watch the signs, then check by putting the answer back in.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** subtracting the coefficient, so 3x = 12 gives x = 9. **Fix:** x is being multiplied, not added to. Divide: x = 4.
- **Mistake:** losing the sign, so −4x = 20 gives x = 5. **Fix:** a positive divided by a negative is negative. x = −5.
- **Mistake:** multiplying by the fraction instead of dividing by it, so (2/3)x = 8 gives x = 16/3. **Fix:** multiply by the reciprocal, 3/2, which gives x = 12.

## Where it shows up

- **Refuel and escort** — burn rate times minutes equals fuel used.
- **Intercept sorties** — closure rate times time equals distance closed.
- **Systems:** Fuel, Engines, Avionics.
- **Real aircraft:** the T-38 climbs at about 33,600 ft per minute at sea level. To gain 16,800 ft you solve 33,600t = 16,800, so t is half a minute.
