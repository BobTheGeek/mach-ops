## What it is

Surface area is how much material wraps round a solid: the total of every face. Unfold the solid flat and it is just a set of rectangles to add up.

**Key idea: Surface area is the total area of ALL the faces. Use a net, or S = 2B + Ph: two bases plus the sides.**

## How to solve it

1. Count the faces. A box has six. A triangular prism has two triangles and three rectangles.
2. Use 2B + Ph for any prism: B is the face that repeats, P is the distance round it, h is how far the solid runs.
3. Count BOTH bases. Forgetting one, or missing a side face, is how this goes wrong most often.
4. Check you were asked for area and not volume. Area covers the outside; volume fills the inside.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** leaving a face out of the total. **Fix:** count them off the net before you add. Six for a box, five for a triangular prism.
- **Mistake:** computing the volume instead. **Fix:** volume multiplies all three dimensions together; surface area adds areas up. The units tell you which you did.
- **Mistake:** counting only one base, giving B + Ph. **Fix:** a prism has a base at each end. Both of them need covering.
