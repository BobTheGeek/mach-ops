## What it is

Mean, median and mode are three different answers to "what is a typical value". They usually differ, and which one is honest depends on the data.

**Key idea: Mean = total ÷ count. Median = the middle of the SORTED list. Mode = the most frequent value. An outlier pulls the mean, not the median.**

## How to solve it

1. Sort the values smallest to largest first. The median is meaningless until you do, and sorting makes the mode easy to see.
2. Mean: add them all up and divide by how many there are. Count them again before you divide.
3. Median: take the middle one. With an even count there are two middles, so average them.
4. Mode: the value that appears MOST often. A set where nothing repeats has no mode at all, and that is a real answer.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** taking the middle of the list as written, so the median of 9, 2, 5 comes out as 2. **Fix:** sort first. The middle of 2, 5, 9 is 5.
- **Mistake:** dividing by one too few, usually by miscounting. **Fix:** count the values a second time before you divide, and check the mean sits between the smallest and the largest.
- **Mistake:** giving the largest value as the mode. **Fix:** mode is about how OFTEN, not how big. In 3, 3, 9 the mode is 3.

## Where it shows up

- **Debrief** — your typical sortie time across a unit.
- **Hangar** — kills per pilot across the squadron.
- **Systems:** Avionics, Engines.
- **Real aircraft:** a squadron's average sortie length is a mean; the sortie in the middle of the list is the median, and one aborted flight moves only the first of them.
