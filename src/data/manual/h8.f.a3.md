## What it is

Linear means one thing: a constant rate of change. Equal steps in x give equal steps in y, and the graph comes out straight.

**Key idea: Linear means a CONSTANT rate of change. Equal steps in x give equal steps in y, and the graph is a straight line. y = x² is not linear.**

## How to solve it

1. Linear means a constant rate of change: equal steps in x give equal steps in y.
2. In a table, subtract each y from the next. All the same means linear; growing or shrinking means not.
3. In an equation, x must be plain: no square, no cube, no x on the bottom, no absolute value.
4. In y = mx + b, m is the rate — how much y changes for each 1 of x — and b is where it starts.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** calling any pattern linear. **Fix:** 1, 4, 9, 16 has a pattern, but the steps are 3, 5, 7. A pattern is not the same as a constant rate.
- **Mistake:** calling a curve linear because it looks straight over a short stretch. **Fix:** check the numbers, not the picture. Over a small range almost anything looks straight.

## Where it shows up

- **Sortie HUD** — altitude against time in a steady climb.
- **Debrief** — the same graph during a stall, which is not linear at all.
- **Systems:** Engines, Avionics.
- **Real aircraft:** fuel burn is close to linear at a steady cruise and nothing like it during a climb.
