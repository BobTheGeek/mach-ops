## What it is

This is the one rule in the chapter that surprises people. Multiply or divide an inequality by a negative number and the symbol reverses. Nothing else reverses it.

**Key idea: divide or multiply both sides. If you divide or multiply by a NEGATIVE, reverse the inequality symbol.**

## How to solve it

1. Find what x is being multiplied or divided by, and do the opposite to both sides.
2. Before you write the answer, look at the sign of the thing you just divided or multiplied by.
3. If it was negative, reverse the symbol. If it was positive, leave it alone — a negative somewhere else in the problem does not count.
4. Write the boundary, then test a number from your shaded side to make sure it really works.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** not reversing when dividing by a negative, so −2x > 10 gives x > −5. **Fix:** test it. x = 0 gives 0 > 10, which is false. The answer is x < −5.
- **Mistake:** reversing because the number on the other side is negative, so 2x > −10 gives x < −5. **Fix:** only the thing you divide BY matters. Two is positive, so the symbol stays: x > −5.

## Where it shows up

- **Intercept sorties** — a descent rate against a floor: −500t > −6,000.
- **Refuel and escort** — how long you can burn before hitting reserve.
- **Systems:** Engines, Fuel, Avionics.
- **Real aircraft:** descending at 4,000 ft per minute from 20,000 ft, staying above 4,000 ft means −4,000t > −16,000, so t < 4 minutes.
