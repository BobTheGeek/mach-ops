## What it is

Solving an inequality works exactly like solving an equation, with one thing to remember: adding or subtracting never changes which way the symbol points.

**Key idea: add or subtract on both sides just like an equation. The symbol does not flip for adding or subtracting.**

## How to solve it

1. Treat it exactly like an equation. Whatever you do to one side, do to the other.
2. Undo the adding or subtracting to get x on its own.
3. Leave the symbol alone. Adding and subtracting never flip it. Only multiplying or dividing by a negative does that, and that is the next skill.
4. Work out the boundary, draw the circle, and shade toward every value that works.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** flipping the symbol after subtracting, out of habit. **Fix:** nothing about adding or subtracting reverses an inequality. Check with a number: if x + 3 > 10 then x > 7, and 8 works.
- **Mistake:** shading the wrong way when the answer reads 5 < x. **Fix:** rewrite it with x first. 5 < x is the same as x > 5, so shade right.

## Where it shows up

- **Briefing** — fuel that must stay above reserve.
- **Intercept sorties** — altitude margin above the terrain.
- **Systems:** Fuel, Avionics.
- **Real aircraft:** if your altitude plus a 1,000 ft margin must stay above 5,000 ft, then x + 1,000 > 5,000 and you must be above 4,000 ft.
