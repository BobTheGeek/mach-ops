## What it is

Three squadrons of (x fighters + 4 tankers) is three lots of each: 3x fighters and 12 tankers. That is the **distributive property**, and it is why an area model works — the outside number multiplies every part of the inside.

**Key idea: multiply the outside number by EVERY term inside, including the sign. −3(x − 4) = −3x + 12.**

## How to solve it

1. Look at what is outside the brackets. That number multiplies everything inside, not just the first thing you see.
2. Multiply the outside number by the variable term, taking its sign with it.
3. Multiply the outside number by the constant. Remember a negative times a negative is positive, which is where −3(x − 4) turns into −3x **plus** 12.
4. Write the two results together, and combine with anything left outside the brackets.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** multiplying only the first term, so −3(x − 4) comes out as −3x − 4. **Fix:** every term inside gets multiplied. Draw an arrow from the outside number to each one before you start.
- **Mistake:** not carrying the sign in, so −(x + 5) comes out as −x + 5. **Fix:** a minus in front of brackets is −1 multiplying everything. −(x + 5) = −x − 5.
- **Mistake:** multiplying only the constant, so 3(2x + 4) comes out as 2x + 12. **Fix:** the variable term gets multiplied too: 6x + 12.

## Where it shows up

- **Briefing** — the total weight of several identical racks.
- **Hangar** — the area of a bay laid out as a grid.
- **Systems:** Weapons, Fuel.
- **Real aircraft:** three T-38s each carrying (f litres of fuel + 200 lb of crew) is 3f + 600 for the flight.
