## What it is

The percent proportion puts a percent problem into the shape you already know from Chapter 5: two equal ratios. Whichever of the three numbers is missing, the setup is the same.

**Key idea: part / whole = percent / 100. Identify the whole first: it is whatever the percent is OF.**

## How to solve it

1. Find the WHOLE. It is whatever the percent is taken of, and it is not always the biggest number in the sentence.
2. Set up part over whole equals percent over 100.
3. Cross multiply and solve for whichever one is missing.
4. Write the answer, with a % sign only if a percent was what was asked for.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** putting the part where the whole goes. **Fix:** read the sentence for the word 'of'. What follows it is the whole.
- **Mistake:** using 25 instead of 25/100. **Fix:** in this setup the percent sits over 100. That 100 is what turns it back into a fraction.

## Where it shows up

- **Debrief** — what percent of your missiles hit.
- **Hangar** — what fraction of the squadron is ready to fly.
- **Systems:** Weapons, Shields.
- **Real aircraft:** a two-seat T-38 with one seat filled is 50% crewed; the whole is 2, not the number of aircraft.
