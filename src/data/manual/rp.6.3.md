## What it is

The percent equation is the percent proportion tidied up into one line: part = percent (as a decimal) × whole. Tax, tip, commission and fees are all this same equation wearing different clothes.

**Key idea: part = percent (as a decimal) × whole. Tax and tip are ADDED to the price.**

## How to solve it

1. Turn the percent into a decimal first. 8% becomes 0.08. This is the step that goes wrong most often.
2. Multiply the decimal by the whole. That gives you the part: the tax, the tip, the fee or the commission.
3. Read the question again. If it asks for the TOTAL, add the part to the price. If it asks for the tax alone, you already have it.
4. Round money to the nearest cent, and write the units.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** multiplying by 8 instead of 0.08, which makes the answer 100 times too big. **Fix:** 8% of $20 is about $1.60, not $160. Move the point two places left before you multiply.
- **Mistake:** giving the tax when the question asked for the total. **Fix:** underline what is being asked. Tax is added ON TOP of the price.
- **Mistake:** adding 8 to the price instead of 8% of it. **Fix:** a percent is a share of the price, so it depends on the price. 8% of $20 is $1.60; 8% of $200 is $16.

## Where it shows up

- **Hangar** — missile price plus tax at the base shop.
- **Debrief** — pilot commission per confirmed kill.
- **Systems:** Weapons, Credits ledger.
- **Real aircraft:** a hangar fee quoted as 6% of the parts bill is 0.06 × the bill, added to it.
