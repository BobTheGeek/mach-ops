## What it is

A proportional relationship drawn on a grid is a straight line through the origin. The steepness of that line is k, and you can read it straight off the graph.

**Key idea: a proportional graph is a straight line through (0, 0). k is the y value at x = 1, the point (1, k). The point (x, y) means 'x of this gives y of that'.**

## How to solve it

1. Check the line goes through the origin, (0, 0). A straight line that starts somewhere else is not proportional.
2. Find the point where x is 1. Its y value is k.
3. Write the equation as y = kx. Nothing is added, because adding would lift the line off the origin.
4. To read any point, say it in words: x of the bottom quantity gives y of the side quantity.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** calling any straight line proportional. **Fix:** look at where it crosses the side axis. If it crosses anywhere but zero, it is not proportional.
- **Mistake:** reading k upside down, taking x over y. **Fix:** k is the height at x = 1. Go across one, then read up.
- **Mistake:** swapping what x and y mean when explaining a point. **Fix:** read the axis labels out loud before you answer.

## Where it shows up

- **Briefing** — the fuel-versus-distance graph on the tactical display.
- **Intercept sorties** — altitude gained against minutes.
- **Systems:** Fuel, Avionics, Credits ledger.
- **Real aircraft:** the T-38 climbs at about 33,600 ft per minute at sea level. Plotted against minutes that is a line through the origin with k = 33,600.
