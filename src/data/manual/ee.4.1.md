## What it is

An equation is a balance. Whatever you do to one side you must do to the other, or it tips. To get x on its own you undo whatever has been done to it.

**Key idea: do the inverse operation to BOTH sides, then check by substituting back.**

## How to solve it

1. Look at what is being done to x. Here it is being added to, or taken away from.
2. Do the opposite. If something was added, subtract it. If something was taken away, add it.
3. Do it to BOTH sides. That is the whole rule: the two sides stay equal only if they change together.
4. Work out the answer, then check it by putting it back into the original equation.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** changing one side and leaving the other. **Fix:** a balance only stays level if both pans change. Write the same operation under both sides before you work anything out.
- **Mistake:** repeating the operation instead of inverting it, so x + 7 = 12 gives x = 19. **Fix:** you are undoing it. Seven was added, so take seven away: x = 5.

## Where it shows up

- **Briefing** — fuel plus reserve equals the total you loaded.
- **Intercept sorties** — starting altitude plus a climb equals your target.
- **Systems:** Fuel, Avionics.
- **Real aircraft:** if your landing fuel plus a 500 lb reserve must total 2,000 lb, then x + 500 = 2,000 and you need 1,500 lb.
