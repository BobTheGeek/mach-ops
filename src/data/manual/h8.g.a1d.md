## What it is

A dilation resizes a figure from a centre point. Every distance from that centre is multiplied by the same number, the scale factor k.

**Key idea: A dilation MULTIPLIES every distance from the centre by k. k > 1 enlarges, 0 < k < 1 shrinks. From the origin, (x, y) becomes (kx, ky).**

## How to solve it

1. A dilation MULTIPLIES. It never adds: (3, 4) at a factor of 2 becomes (6, 8), not (5, 6).
2. From the origin, multiply BOTH coordinates by k.
3. To find k, divide an image length by its matching original length. Bigger than 1 means it grew.
4. Every LENGTH scales by k, so the perimeter does too — but AREA scales by k SQUARED, because both sides grow at once.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** adding k to each coordinate instead of multiplying. **Fix:** a dilation from the origin stretches everything away from it. Doubling (3, 4) gives (6, 8).
- **Mistake:** scaling an area by k. **Fix:** area needs two lengths, and both of them grow. Multiply by k twice.

## Where it shows up

- **Hangar** — a 1:72 model against the full airframe.
- **Sortie HUD** — zooming the tactical map.
- **Systems:** Avionics.
- **Real aircraft:** zooming a moving map by 2 shows a quarter of the ground, because area scales by the square.
