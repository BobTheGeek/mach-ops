## What it is

Once you trust a² + b² = c², you can find any missing side of a right triangle from the other two. The only care needed is which side is missing.

**Key idea: Missing hypotenuse: c = √(a² + b²). Missing leg: a = √(c² − b²). Take the square root at the END.**

## How to solve it

1. Spot the hypotenuse: opposite the right angle, and the longest side. Everything else is a leg.
2. For the HYPOTENUSE, square both legs and ADD.
3. For a LEG, square the hypotenuse and SUBTRACT the leg you have. Adding there would give a leg longer than the hypotenuse, which cannot happen.
4. Take the square root at the end. c² is not c.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** stopping at a² + b² and calling it c. **Fix:** that is c SQUARED. 9 + 16 is 25, so c is 5.
- **Mistake:** adding when the missing side is a leg. **Fix:** the hypotenuse is the biggest. If your answer for a leg came out bigger than it, you added instead of subtracting.

## Where it shows up

- **Briefing** — 9 km east then 12 km north: 15 km direct to the target.
- **Hangar** — the diagonal across a hangar floor.
- **Systems:** Avionics.
- **Real aircraft:** a diversion to an alternate field is almost always this calculation, done on the direct leg.
