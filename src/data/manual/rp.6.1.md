## What it is

Percent means 'per hundred'. A percent, a decimal and a fraction are three ways of writing the same amount, and being able to swap between them is what makes the rest of Chapter 6 work.

**Key idea: percent means 'per 100'. Percent to decimal: divide by 100, moving the point two places LEFT. Decimal to percent: multiply by 100.**

## How to solve it

1. To go from a percent to a decimal, divide by 100: move the decimal point two places left. 22% becomes 0.22.
2. To go the other way, multiply by 100: move the point two places right.
3. To go from a fraction, divide the top by the bottom to get a decimal, then move the point.
4. Watch the small and large ones. 0.5% is half of one percent, which is 0.005; 250% is two and a half times the whole thing.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** moving the point the wrong way, so 22% becomes 2200. **Fix:** a percent is always smaller than the number in front of the sign suggests. 22% is about a fifth, so it must be 0.22.
- **Mistake:** reading 0.5% as 50%. **Fix:** 0.5% is half of one percent, a hundredth of 50%.
- **Mistake:** writing 250% as 0.25. **Fix:** anything over 100% is more than the whole. 250% is 2.5.

## Where it shows up

- **Sortie HUD** — shield and fuel gauges, which show the same number as a bar, a percent and a fraction.
- **Hangar** — discounts written as percents.
- **Systems:** Fuel, Shields.
- **Real aircraft:** the T-38 is listed at Mach 1.08, which is 108% of the speed of sound.
