## What it is

A scatter with a trend in it can be summed up by one straight line. The line of fit runs through the middle of the cloud, not through the points.

**Key idea: A line of fit runs through the MIDDLE of the cloud, with about as many points above it as below. Judge the fit by how close the points stay to it.**

## How to solve it

1. A line of fit runs through the middle of the cloud, with about as many points above it as below.
2. It does not have to touch any of the points. Hitting the most points is not the same as fitting them all.
3. Judge the fit by how close the points stay to the line. A tight band is a strong fit; a wide scatter is a weak one.
4. Ignore a single stray reading when you place the line. One outlier can drag a careless line right off the trend.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** joining the first and last points. **Fix:** those two are just two readings. A line through them ignores everything in between.
- **Mistake:** aiming for the line that touches the most points. **Fix:** balance matters more than contact. A line through four points with everything else above it is a bad fit.

## Where it shows up

- **Debrief** — lock time against a pilot's hours.
- **Hangar** — fuel burn against payload.
- **Systems:** Avionics, Fuel.
- **Real aircraft:** a performance trend line on a maintenance chart is a line of fit, and one bad reading is not allowed to move it.
