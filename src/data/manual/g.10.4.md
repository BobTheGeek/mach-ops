## What it is

Volume is how much a solid holds. For anything that keeps the same cross-section all the way along — a box, a pipe, a girder — it is one multiplication.

**Key idea: V = B × h: the area of the base times the height. It works for ANY prism; the base is the face that repeats.**

## How to solve it

1. Find the BASE: the face that repeats all the way along. For a box it is the floor; for a triangular prism it is the triangle on the end.
2. Work out that base's area on its own. A triangle is half base times height; a trapezium is half the two parallel sides added, times the gap.
3. Volume is B × h. MULTIPLY the base area by how far the solid runs.
4. Going backwards from a volume, divide by the base area to get the missing length.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** using a face that is not the repeating one as the base. **Fix:** the base is whichever face you would see if you sliced across the solid. For a box any pair works; for anything else it does not.
- **Mistake:** adding the dimensions instead of multiplying. **Fix:** 3 + 4 + 5 is 12; 3 × 4 × 5 is 60. A volume is a multiplication.
- **Mistake:** computing the surface area. **Fix:** volume fills the inside and its units are cubed.

## Where it shows up

- **Hangar** — how many litres a fuel tank holds.
- **Briefing** — cargo bay capacity.
- **Systems:** Fuel.
- **Real aircraft:** fuel is loaded by volume and paid for by weight, so both figures are on every sheet.
