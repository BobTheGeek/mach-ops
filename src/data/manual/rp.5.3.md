## What it is

Two quantities are **proportional** when one is always the same multiple of the other. That multiple is called k, the constant of proportionality. Not every straight-line relationship is proportional.

**Key idea: proportional means every y ÷ x gives the same number k, and (0, 0) fits. Check EVERY row, not just two.**

## How to solve it

1. Divide y by x for every row of the table. Every row, not just the first two: one bad row breaks it.
2. If all the answers match, that shared number is k.
3. Check that (0, 0) works. Something like y = 3x + 2 makes a straight line, but at x = 0 it gives 2, not 0, so it is not proportional.
4. Say yes only if both of those are true.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** calling y = x + 2 proportional because it draws a straight line. **Fix:** proportional means multiplied by, not added to. Test x = 0: a proportional relationship must give 0.
- **Mistake:** checking two rows and stopping. **Fix:** a table can match on the first two rows and break on the third. Check all of them.
- **Mistake:** working k out as x divided by y. **Fix:** k is y divided by x, because the equation is y = kx.

## Where it shows up

- **Briefing** — is the fuel-versus-distance table a steady rate, or is there a fixed start-up burn?
- **Hangar** — credits per mission, with and without a bonus.
- **Systems:** Fuel, Engines, Credits ledger.
- **Real aircraft:** fuel burnt is roughly proportional to distance at a steady cruise. Add a fixed amount for start-up and taxi and it stops being proportional.
