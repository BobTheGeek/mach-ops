## What it is

Dividing integers undoes multiplying them, so it uses the same sign rule. It is how you turn a total into a rate: total altitude lost divided by minutes gives feet per minute.

**Key idea: same sign rule as multiplication. −(p/q) = (−p)/q = p/(−q). You cannot divide by 0.**

## How to solve it

1. Divide the sizes, ignoring the signs for now.
2. Same signs give a positive answer; different signs give a negative one. It is the multiplication rule again, because division is the same fact family.
3. Write the answer with that sign. If it does not divide evenly, leave it as a fraction or an exact decimal rather than rounding.

A minus sign can sit in front of the fraction, on the top, or on the bottom, and all three mean the same value. Two minus signs cancel, and the value is positive.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** using the addition rule, so (−36) ÷ (−3) comes out as −12. **Fix:** same signs give a positive answer. (−36) ÷ (−3) = 12.
- **Mistake:** swapping 0 ÷ n and n ÷ 0. **Fix:** zero divided by anything is 0. Anything divided by zero is undefined, because nothing times 0 gives a number other than 0.
- **Mistake:** thinking −6/2 and 6/(−2) are different numbers. **Fix:** count the minus signs. One minus anywhere makes the value negative; both give a positive.

## Where it shows up

- **Intercept sorties** — average descent rate from a total altitude change.
- **Refuel and escort** — fuel burn per minute from a total burn.
- **Systems:** Engine, Fuel, Credits ledger.
- **Real aircraft:** the SR-71 cruised near 85,000 ft. Descending 68,000 ft over 17 minutes is (−68,000) ÷ 17 = −4,000 ft per minute.
