## What it is

Factoring is distributing run backwards. Instead of opening brackets you put them back, by pulling out the largest thing both terms share. That shared thing is the **greatest common factor**, or GCF.

**Key idea: find the greatest common factor of the terms, divide each term by it, and write GCF(...). Check by distributing back.**

## How to solve it

1. Look at both terms and ask what divides into both of them. List the factors if it helps.
2. Take the **greatest** one. 2 divides into 6x and 12, but so does 6, and 6 is the one you want.
3. Write the GCF outside a set of brackets, then divide each original term by it and write what is left inside.
4. Check by multiplying back out. You should land exactly on what you started with.

## Watch out for

- **Mistake:** pulling out a common factor that is not the greatest, so 6x + 12 becomes 2(3x + 6). **Fix:** it multiplies back correctly, but it is not finished: 3x + 6 still has a 3 in it. Keep going until nothing is left to pull out.
- **Mistake:** losing the constant, so 6x + 12 becomes 6(x). **Fix:** every term goes inside the brackets. Dividing 12 by 6 gives 2, not nothing.
- **Mistake:** getting the sign wrong inside the brackets. **Fix:** check by distributing back. If the signs do not match what you started with, flip the one inside.

## Where it shows up

- **Briefing** — grouping identical loads into equal flights.
- **Refuel and escort** — rewriting a fuel total as so much per tank.
- **Systems:** Weapons, Fuel.
- **Real aircraft:** a flight of four T-38s carrying 4f + 800 lb factors to 4(f + 200): each aircraft carries f litres of fuel plus 200 lb of crew.
