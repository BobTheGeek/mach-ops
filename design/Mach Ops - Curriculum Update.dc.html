1 · WHAT IT IS
A system is two equations that share x and y. Graph both lines; where they cross is the one pair that works for both. Parallel lines never cross, so no solution. Same line, infinitely many.
CROSSING POINT = SOLUTION
2 · HOW TO SOLVE IT
1 · Put both in y = mx + b.
2 · Plot each intercept, then use slope.
3 · Draw both lines to the edges.
4 · Read the crossing; check in both.
DIAGRAM SLOT · MK-2
3 · WORKED EXAMPLE
↻ GENERATED
Your track y = 2x + 1, bogey track y = −x + 7. Merge point?
01 both already y = mx + b
02 you: b = 1, up 2 per 1 · bogey: b = 7, down 1 per 1
03 cross at x = 2 → y = 5
04 = (2, 5) · check: 2(2)+1 = 5 ✓ · −2+7 = 5 ✓
4 · WATCH OUT FOR
Reading the crossing off-grid. Fix: solve algebraically to confirm.
Same slope, different b. Fix: parallel → no solution, not "0".
Swapping x and y in the answer. Fix: (x, y) · east, then north.
