1 · WHAT IT IS
Subtracting an integer is the same as adding its opposite. 8 − (−3) is 8 + 3. The result can be negative, and the order matters.
KEEP · CHANGE · CHANGE
keep the first number, change − to +, change the sign of the second.
2 · HOW TO SOLVE IT
1 · Rewrite the subtraction as adding the opposite.
2 · Same signs: add the sizes, keep the sign.
3 · Different signs: subtract the sizes, take the sign of the bigger one.
4 · Check on a number line: moving right means a positive answer.
3 · WORKED EXAMPLE
GENERATED · NEW
Bogey A is 2,500 ft below you (−2,500). Bogey B is 1,800 ft above you (+1,800). Vertical gap between the bogeys?
01 1,800 − (−2,500)
02 1,800 + 2,500 · keep · change · change
03 same signs → add sizes, keep +
04 = 4,300 FT
4 · WATCH OUT FOR
Two minuses read as minus. Fix: − (−) always becomes +.
Swapping the order. Fix: a − b is not b − a; keep the first number first.
Dropping the answer's sign. Fix: the bigger size decides the sign.

1 · WHAT IT IS
A unit rate compares two quantities where the second one is 1: pounds per 1 minute, miles per 1 hour. You get it by dividing.
RATE = AMOUNT ÷ TIME
or amount ÷ one of anything. The word "per" marks the 1.
2 · HOW TO SOLVE IT
1 · Name the two quantities and their units.
2 · Put the "per one" quantity on the bottom and divide.
3 · Label the answer with "per".
4 · Multiply the unit rate by any new amount to scale up or down.
3 · WORKED EXAMPLE
GENERATED · NEW
You burned 4,200 lb of fuel in 12 minutes. What is the burn rate, and how many minutes does 1,750 lb give you?
01 4,200 LB ÷ 12 MIN
02 = 350 LB PER MIN · unit rate
03 1,750 ÷ 350
04 = 5 MIN
4 · WATCH OUT FOR
Dividing the wrong way. Fix: the thing you want "per one of" goes on the bottom.
Mixing seconds and minutes. Fix: convert first, then divide.
Forgetting the label. Fix: every rate has a "per".
